The one thing you lose
every time you hurry
It's not time.
Time keeps moving whether you rush or not.
The thing you lose...
is presence.
The capacity to be fully here.
Right now. Without your mind already in the next moment.
When you hurry, your brain time-travels.
It projects forward, scanning for threats,
running scenarios, spending attention
on a future that hasn't arrived yet.
Attention is not time.
Attention is life.
To reclaim your attention is to
reclaim your existence from autopilot.
One breath. One moment. One presence
at a time.
You don't need more time.
You need to be here for the time you already have.

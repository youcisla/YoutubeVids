HOW IT WORKS
Why does bread go stale
but crackers don't?
🌾🍞🥖
Both are made from the same stuff.
Flour, water, maybe yeast or oil.
🔬
The secret is starch.
Starch molecules start packed like tiny crystals.
Heat + water melts them into a soft gel.
🧊
Over time, water leaves the gel.
Starch re-crystallises — that's retrogradation.
Bread goes stale.
🍘
Crackers have almost no water.
They start crunchy and stay that way.
No moisture = no starch change.
💧→🌡️
Put a cracker in a humid room?
It goes soft.
The reverse of stale.
🍞✨
Stale bread → toaster = miracle.
The heat melts the starch crystals again.
Thirty seconds of magic.
The secret is water.
It makes bread. It makes it stale.
And it makes it fresh again.
That's your minute of science for today.

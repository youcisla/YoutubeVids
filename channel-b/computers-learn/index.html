AI Pals ✨
How do computers learn?
🧠
When you learn something new —
you practice. When you get it wrong,
your brain says: remember that. Try again.
🤖
A computer learns the exact same way.
Just a lot faster.
And with fewer snack breaks.
First we give the computer lots of examples.
Thousands of pictures of
cats and dogs 🐱🐶
Each time the computer guesses wrong,
a little signal goes back and says:
"Try again."
Slowly, the computer gets better.
This is called training.
It is like homework for computers.
Except they never get bored.
Once trained, AI can see a new picture
and know what it is.
Not magic. Because it practiced. 🎉

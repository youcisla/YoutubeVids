AI Pals ✨
Where does AI live?
📱➡️💬
You ask your phone a question.
Instantly an answer comes back.
But where did it really come from?
🏢
AI does not live inside your phone.
It lives in a giant building
full of computers. Called a data center.
🖥️🖥️🖥️
Thousands of computers,
stacked on top of each other.
All connected. Running all day and night.
Your question travels through the internet
to the data center. The computers find
the answer and send it back —
in under a second.
Some data centers are the size of a
football stadium. They need so much
electricity they have their own power plants.
Your invisible AI friend
lives in a noisy, giant,
power-hungry building far away.
And you talk to it through a wire.

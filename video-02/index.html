Why your brain refuses
to rest
Not because you're lazy.
Because your brain has been trained —
rewarded — for never stopping.
And now it doesn't know how.
Every notification, every task checked off,
every email answered —
releases a small dose of dopamine.
Your brain has been hooked on the reward of busyness.
Rest is not the absence of work.
Rest is a biological requirement —
like sleep, like water, like air.
Stillness is not emptiness.
It is space for your mind to return to itself.
You don't need to earn rest.
You need to allow it.
